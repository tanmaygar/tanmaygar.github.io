## Summary
We propose a mesh-free physics-informed conditional diffusion framework for multi-modal PDEs, including quantum systems (Schrödinger), wave propagation (Helmholtz) and nonlinear dynamics (Gross-Pitaevskii). Training combines a physics loss with a data loss and samples with DDIM. Against baselines, it reduces MSE by 13% and Wasserstein-1 distance by 10%, and avoids mode collapse across 1D and 2D PDE domains.
